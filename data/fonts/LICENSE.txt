Handwriting fonts used to render synthetic prescription training data.

All from the google/fonts repository under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:
  Caveat (Pablo Impallari, Rodrigo Fuenzalida)
  DancingScript (Impallari Type)
  IndieFlower (Kimberly Geswein)
  Kalam Regular & Bold (Indian Type Foundry)
  PatrickHand (Patrick Wagesreiter)
  ReenieBeanie (James Grieshaber)
  ShadowsIntoLight (Kimberly Geswein)

OFL permits bundling and derived works with attribution. The cluster also
provides Z003 (URW chancery) from urw-base35, which ships under the AFPL/GPL
font exception — used only as an additional rendering family.
